Basement Ready: could your basement be a home?

Film your basement once on your phone, and Basement Ready tells you whether it can become a legal second home anywhere in Ontario. Meta's models find the windows, stairs and ducts, measure the space from ordinary video and check it against the Ontario Building Code. Then a guide takes you through the answer, the design and the build: the permit set, the money you can apply for, and who builds it.

Try the example: https://www.basementready.com
Code: https://github.com/Dbar2/basement-ready

Built during the Meta AI Developer Hackathon, from an empty repo on Oct 28.

Meta models used: SAM 3.1 (sam-3.1), Muse Spark 1.3 (muse-spark-1.3), Muse Wonder 1.0 (muse-wonder-1.0), Muse Image 1.0 (muse-image-1.0), Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0 (muse-voice-transcribe-1.0), MapAnything and DINOv2 (open Meta models, run on Modal).
