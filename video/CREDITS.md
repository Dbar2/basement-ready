## Music

`public/audio/music.wav` is the one music file (now the ElevenLabs candy-a track). The edit (`src/Cut.tsx`) lands its first drop (39.65 s, `take.json` `music2.drop`) on "And the answer comes first", playing 4 bars of the calm intro twice, cut on bar lines, so the track starts with the video. To swap in another track, replace the file and set `music2.drop` and `music2.bar` for it. The mix ducks it under the voice; `tools/finish.sh` masters the mix to -14 LUFS.

Earlier cuts used "New Day" by Lee Rosevere (CC BY 4.0, `tools/music/fetch.sh`); that track is no longer in the video.
